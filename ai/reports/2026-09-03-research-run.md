## The three most important things learned
1. The CAPI values (150/100/50/10) are labels, not levers, on a Leads objective.
   Value optimization is not offered there. The real quality lever is Conversion Leads
   with CRM stage events, which needs about 250 leads a month per account, or a custom
   valued event under a value-optimizing objective on larger accounts.
2. The template carries verified legal exposure: no consent line on the survey form,
   hardcoded named testimonials and a scarcity line deployed to every client.
3. Organic informational content now pays in AI citations first and clicks second; the
   money query still pays in clicks; swapped-city pages are a liability; the pooled
   client dataset is the one asset competitors on Carrot cannot copy.
